Offshore Operations

Offshore operations bring together specialist personnel, vessels and equipment across drilling, production, construction and marine activities.

Offshore Operations

  • Offshore DrillingWell construction and drilling support.
  • Offshore ProductionOperation and monitoring of offshore production facilities.
  • Offshore ConstructionConstruction work for offshore structures and facilities.
  • Offshore MaintenancePlanned and corrective maintenance offshore.
  • Offshore InstallationInstallation of offshore structures and systems.
  • Hook-up & CommissioningConnecting systems and preparing facilities for operation.
  • Well InterventionIntervention and service activities on existing wells.
  • Workover OperationsWell service and workover support.
  • DecommissioningPlanning and support for retirement of offshore assets.
  • Subsea OperationsInstallation, inspection and maintenance of subsea systems.
  • Offshore LogisticsCoordination of personnel, materials and equipment.
  • Marine OperationsVessel coordination and marine support for offshore work.

Offshore Rigs

  • Jack-Up RigsMobile drilling units used for offshore well operations.
  • Semi-Submersible RigsFloating units designed to support offshore drilling.
  • DrillshipsShips equipped and operated for offshore drilling work.
  • Platform RigsDrilling rigs operating from offshore platforms.
  • Tender-Assisted Drilling UnitsTender units provide support for platform drilling operations.
  • Mobile Offshore Drilling Units (MODUs)Mobile units used for offshore drilling operations.

Offshore Equipment

  • Drilling DerrickSupports drilling equipment and tubular handling.
  • Top DriveRotates the drill string during drilling operations.
  • DrawworksProvides hoisting control for drilling operations.
  • Mud PumpsCirculate drilling fluid through the well system.
  • Blowout Preventer (BOP)Well-control equipment used to seal and control a well.
  • WellheadSurface equipment supporting and sealing the well.
  • Mud SystemManages drilling-fluid storage, treatment and circulation.
  • Offshore CranesHandle loads between vessels and offshore facilities.
  • WinchesProvide controlled line handling and pulling.
  • Mooring SystemsMaintain position using anchors, lines or related equipment.
  • Dynamic PositioningComputer-controlled systems help vessels maintain position.
  • ROV SystemsRemotely operated vehicles support subsea tasks.
  • GeneratorsSupply electrical power for offshore systems.
  • CompressorsProvide compressed air or gas for operational systems.
  • PumpsMove fluids for process and utility duties.
  • Fire & Gas SystemsDetect hazards and support protective responses.
  • Emergency Shutdown SystemsBring designated processes to a safe state during emergencies.
  • LifeboatsProvide means of evacuation in an emergency.
  • HelideckDesignated landing area for helicopter operations.

Offshore Job Categories

These are role categories, not advertised vacancies. Openings and requirements depend on the specific project and client.

Master / Captain

Leading vessel operations with safety, navigation and crew management expertise.

Chief Officer

Supporting vessel operations, deck management and onboard safety procedures.

Chief Engineer

Managing marine engineering systems, machinery and technical operations.

ETO

Handling electrical, electronic and automation systems across marine operations.

Driller

Operating and monitoring drilling systems during offshore drilling operations.

Roustabout

Supporting deck, drilling and general offshore operations with a strong safety focus.

Floorman

Supporting drilling-floor activities, equipment handling and rig operations.

Derrickman

Supporting drilling operations and derrick-related activities on offshore rigs.

AB / Able Seaman

Supporting deck operations, maintenance, mooring and safe vessel operations.

OS / Ordinary Seaman

Assisting with deck operations, maintenance and general marine duties.

Oiler / Wiper

Supporting engine-room operations, machinery maintenance and housekeeping duties.

Offshore Rigger

Supporting lifting, rigging and material-handling operations on offshore facilities.

Crane Operator

Operating offshore lifting equipment safely and efficiently in accordance with site procedures.

Mechanical Technician

Maintaining and supporting mechanical equipment across offshore and onshore operations.

Electrical Technician

Supporting electrical systems, equipment maintenance and troubleshooting.

Instrument Technician

Maintaining instrumentation, control systems and process measurement equipment.

HSE Officer

Supporting health, safety and environmental standards across marine and offshore operations.

Pipe Fitter

Carrying out pipe installation, maintenance and fabrication work across industrial projects.

Welder / Fabricator

Performing welding and fabrication activities for marine, offshore and industrial projects.

Cook / Steward

Supporting catering, accommodation and welfare services for offshore and marine personnel.
